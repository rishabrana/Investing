# Stock Screeners: Momentum vs Value Investing

## Overview
This document outlines the key metrics and criteria for two distinct trading strategies: Momentum/Short-term Trading and Value Investing/Long-term Trading.

---

## 1. Momentum / Short-Term Trading Screener

### Strategy Description
Momentum trading capitalizes on existing market trends and price movements. The strategy identifies stocks with strong recent performance, high trading activity, and technical indicators suggesting continued upward momentum.

### Top 10 Metrics & Criteria

#### 1. **Relative Strength Index (RSI)**
- **Target Range**: 50-70 (bullish momentum without being overbought)
- **Rationale**: Identifies stocks with strong momentum that aren't yet in overbought territory
- **Avoid**: RSI > 80 (overbought risk) or RSI < 30 (downtrend)

#### 2. **Price Rate of Change (ROC)**
- **Target**: Positive ROC over multiple timeframes (1-week, 1-month, 3-month)
- **Ideal**: 5-20% monthly gain
- **Rationale**: Confirms consistent upward price momentum across different periods

#### 3. **Moving Average Convergence/Divergence (MACD)**
- **Signal**: MACD line crossing above signal line
- **Ideal**: Positive and increasing histogram
- **Rationale**: Indicates strengthening bullish momentum

#### 4. **Volume Surge**
- **Target**: Current volume > 1.5x average 20-day volume
- **Ideal**: 2-3x average volume on up days
- **Rationale**: High volume confirms institutional interest and validates price movements

#### 5. **Price Above Moving Averages**
- **Criteria**: Price > 20-day MA > 50-day MA > 200-day MA (golden alignment)
- **Minimum**: Price above 50-day MA with upward slope
- **Rationale**: Confirms established uptrend across multiple timeframes

#### 6. **52-Week High Proximity**
- **Target**: Trading within 15-20% of 52-week high
- **Ideal**: Making new 52-week highs
- **Rationale**: Strong momentum stocks often break into new price territories

#### 7. **Average Daily Volume**
- **Minimum**: 500,000 shares/day (preferably 1M+)
- **Rationale**: Ensures liquidity for quick entry/exit without significant slippage

#### 8. **Beta (Volatility)**
- **Target**: Beta > 1.2
- **Ideal Range**: 1.3-2.0
- **Rationale**: Higher beta stocks provide greater short-term profit potential

#### 9. **Earnings Momentum**
- **Criteria**: Positive earnings surprises in last 2 quarters
- **Ideal**: Upward earnings estimate revisions
- **Rationale**: Fundamental catalyst that can sustain technical momentum

#### 10. **Institutional Ownership Changes**
- **Target**: Increasing institutional ownership (last quarter)
- **Ideal**: 5-10% increase in institutional holdings
- **Rationale**: Smart money accumulation often precedes sustained momentum

### Additional Filters
- **Market Cap**: Minimum $500M (mid-cap or larger for liquidity)
- **Sector Rotation**: Focus on sectors showing relative strength vs. market
- **News Catalysts**: Recent positive news, upgrades, or product launches

## 2. Value Investing / Long-Term Trading Screener

### Strategy Description
Value investing focuses on identifying fundamentally strong companies trading below their intrinsic value. The strategy emphasizes financial health, profitability, and sustainable competitive advantages with a multi-year holding period.

### Top 10 Metrics & Criteria

#### 1. **Price-to-Earnings Ratio (P/E)**
- **Target**: P/E < Industry Average AND < Market P/E
- **Ideal**: P/E ratio 30-50% below sector median
- **Avoid**: Extremely low P/E (<5) may signal fundamental problems
- **Rationale**: Identifies potentially undervalued stocks relative to earnings power

#### 2. **Price-to-Book Ratio (P/B)**
- **Target**: P/B < 3.0 (preferably < 1.5)
- **Ideal**: P/B < 1.0 for deep value opportunities
- **Rationale**: Stocks trading near or below book value offer downside protection

#### 3. **Free Cash Flow (FCF) Yield**
- **Target**: FCF Yield > 5%
- **Ideal**: FCF Yield > 8%
- **Calculation**: (Free Cash Flow / Market Cap) × 100
- **Rationale**: Measures actual cash generation relative to valuation

#### 4. **Return on Equity (ROE)**
- **Minimum**: ROE > 15%
- **Ideal**: Consistently > 20% over 5 years
- **Rationale**: Indicates efficient use of shareholder capital and competitive advantage

#### 5. **Debt-to-Equity Ratio**
- **Target**: D/E < 0.5 (conservative)
- **Maximum**: D/E < 1.0 (industry-dependent)
- **Rationale**: Lower debt reduces financial risk and provides stability during downturns

#### 6. **Current Ratio**
- **Minimum**: Current Ratio > 1.5
- **Ideal**: Current Ratio > 2.0
- **Rationale**: Ensures strong liquidity and ability to meet short-term obligations

#### 7. **Dividend Yield & History**
- **Target**: Dividend Yield > 2.5%
- **Critical**: 5+ years of consistent or growing dividends
- **Ideal**: Dividend aristocrat (25+ years of dividend growth)
- **Rationale**: Demonstrates financial stability and shareholder-friendly management

#### 8. **PEG Ratio (Price/Earnings-to-Growth)**
- **Target**: PEG < 1.0
- **Ideal**: PEG < 0.75
- **Calculation**: (P/E Ratio) / (Annual EPS Growth Rate)
- **Rationale**: Identifies growth at a reasonable price (GARP)

#### 9. **Operating Margin**
- **Minimum**: Operating Margin > 10%
- **Ideal**: Operating Margin > 15% and stable/improving
- **Rationale**: Indicates pricing power, efficiency, and competitive moat

#### 10. **Earnings Stability & Growth**
- **Criteria**: Positive earnings in 8 of last 10 years
- **Growth**: 5-year average EPS growth > 5%
- **Rationale**: Consistent profitability indicates business resilience and quality

### Additional Filters
- **Market Cap**: Minimum $2B (reduces small-cap risk)
- **Piotroski F-Score**: Score ≥ 7 out of 9 (financial strength test)
- **Insider Ownership**: > 5% insider ownership (management confidence)
- **Institutional Ownership**: 40-80% (validates investment thesis without overcrowding)
- **Price Performance**: May be underperforming market short-term (contrarian opportunity)

## Implementation Strategy

### Momentum Screener Workflow
1. Start with technical filters (MA alignment, RSI, volume)
2. Layer in momentum indicators (ROC, MACD)
3. Validate with fundamental catalysts (earnings surprises)
4. Confirm with institutional activity
5. Review daily/weekly for fresh opportunities

### Value Screener Workflow
1. Begin with valuation metrics (P/E, P/B, PEG)
2. Filter for financial health (D/E, Current Ratio)
3. Assess profitability (ROE, Operating Margin, FCF)
4. Verify consistency (earnings stability, dividend history)
5. Deep-dive fundamental analysis on shortlist
6. Review quarterly for new opportunities

## Key Differences Summary

| Aspect | Momentum Trading | Value Investing |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Time Horizon** | Days to months | Years to decades |
| **Primary Focus** | Price action & trends | Intrinsic value & fundamentals |
| **Risk Tolerance** | Higher (quick stops) | Lower (margin of safety) |
| **Volume Importance** | Critical | Less important |
| **Technical Analysis** | Primary driver | Secondary consideration |
| **Fundamental Analysis** | Validation only | Primary driver |
| **Typical Holding Period** | 1-12 weeks | 3-10+ years |
| **Win Rate** | Lower (40-50%) | Higher (60-70%) |
| **Profit per Trade** | Higher R:R ratio | Steady appreciation |

## Notes for Implementation

### Data Requirements
- **Real-time data**: Essential for momentum; daily EOD sufficient for value
- **Historical data**: 1-year minimum for momentum; 5-10 years ideal for value
- **Fundamental data**: Quarterly updates sufficient for value; earnings dates critical for momentum

### Rebalancing Frequency
- **Momentum**: Weekly screening, daily watchlist monitoring
- **Value**: Quarterly screening, monthly portfolio review

### Position Sizing
- **Momentum**: Smaller positions (2-5% per trade) due to higher risk
- **Value**: Larger positions (5-10% per holding) with high conviction

### Exit Strategy
- **Momentum**: Strict stop-losses (5-8%), take profits at 15-25%
- **Value**: Sell when price reaches intrinsic value or fundamentals deteriorate
